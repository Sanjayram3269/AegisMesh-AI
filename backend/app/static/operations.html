What this lab proves—and its limits

These are controlled integration checks with synthetic data. They do not measure universal attack prevention, real-world detection accuracy or production readiness. The SQLite audit chain detects local record modification, but a host administrator with the signing key can rewrite history. This deployment serves one organization.
